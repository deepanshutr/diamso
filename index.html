The challenge

Three risks protocol teams carry.

Teams change protocol code faster than anyone reviews it. Miss one check, and users can lose funds.

Teams grow modules without a written design, so every new engineer relearns the system from the code.

When teams upgrade a chain or a library, they rarely test what happens to stored state.
