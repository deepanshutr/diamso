The challenge

Three risks protocol teams carry.

  1. Teams change protocol code faster than anyone reviews it. Miss one check, and users can lose funds.

  2. Teams grow modules without a written design, so every new engineer relearns the system from the code.

  3. When teams upgrade a chain or a library, they rarely test what happens to stored state.

What we do

Four ways to work with us. Hire us for one hard problem, not for extra hands on a sprint.

  • Consult

    Bring the hardest question about your chain to a video call. We confirm within 1 business day and send the link after payment.

  • Advise

    We review designs and audit code the way an attacker reads it. For each finding we name the file, the risk and the fix, ranked by severity.

  • Build

    We build chain modules, libraries and developer tools on a fixed scope. You receive the code, its tests and its documentation.

  • Maintain

    We review your pull requests, upgrade your chain and libraries, and patch issues as they appear, on a monthly retainer.

Practice

Framework

  1. A walkthrough of your system and the people who run it.

  2. A one-page problem statement you sign off.

  3. The work split into parts we can ship separately.

  4. An agreed scope and a definition of done.

  5. A design with its assumptions tested.

  6. Reviewed, tested code, shipped with documentation.
