Privacy

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from another website until you click a video or a booking link. We receive personal data only when you book or pay, as described below.

This policy follows India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

When you play a video, YouTube (Google) receives your request under Google's privacy policy. Until you press play, no request goes to YouTube.

When you book a call through Cal.com, Cal.com processes the details you enter (name, email, company, GSTIN and your notes) under its own privacy policy, and sends them to hello@diamso.com. When you pay, your UPI app and bank process the payment. We receive the amount, the time, the payment reference and the payer name shown by the bank.

We use your details only to deliver the service you paid for, to send your GST invoice and to keep the records Indian tax law requires. We do not sell or share them. Invoices and payment records are kept for as long as GST law requires. Ask at hello@diamso.com to see, correct or delete anything we are not required to keep.
