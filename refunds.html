Refund and Cancellation

Last updated: 30 September 2026.

Refunds

  • Duplicate or accidental payments are refunded in full to the original payment method within 7 days of your request.
  • Cancel or move a call 24 hours or more before its start at no cost. A cancellation made 24 hours or more before the start is refunded in full within 7 days.
  • A call cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice, or missed without notice, is not refunded, but can be moved once to another time.
  • If we cancel or cannot hold a call, you choose a new time or a full refund within 7 days.
  • Calls that have taken place are not refunded.
  • Coffee payments for open source maintenance are not refunded, except duplicate or accidental payments, and except that if the commits are not delivered within 30 days of payment, you get a full refund within 7 days.
  • Quoted work follows the refund terms in its written quote.

Request a refund at hello@diamso.com with the payment reference. We answer within 2 business days.
